# MAEOS project-adoption template

## Classification

Choose one: `ACTIVE PROJECT`, `REVIVED LEGACY PROJECT`, `NEW PROJECT`, or `ARCHIVED / PRE-MAEOS PROJECT`.

## Authority and scope

Record the accepted project specification, current pointer, branch/worktree, existing project policy, and any stricter safety requirements. A project may tighten MAEOS; it may not replace the Context Vault as global authority.

## Adoption decision

For active or revived work, first audit existing orchestration overrides and create a bounded amendment if stale limits conflict. Preserve project-specific locks, migration/release rules, and unexplained state. Archived projects remain untouched until revival.

## Verification and rollback

Record profile/gate compatibility, one-writer evidence, required tests, review trigger, exact changed files, and a narrow reversal. Do not resume product work until adoption is verified.
