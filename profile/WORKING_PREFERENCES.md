# Working Preferences

- Earl generally works better with flexible structure than rigid scheduling.
- Plans should emphasize priorities, recovery paths, and next actions.
- Sudden changes should be handled by adapting the plan rather than treating the entire plan as failed.
- Recommendations should help restore clarity and control.
- Large tasks should be divided into focused workstreams.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and excessive preliminary questioning.
- When a task is large, surface meaningful progress and concrete next steps.
- Keep plans practical and nonclinical.
