# MAEOS delegation and context isolation

Use profitable delegation, not solo-first or swarm-first behavior. Keep root-owned architecture, owner decisions, protected boundaries, and immediately deterministic evidence with Sol. A worker packet is self-contained: authority, objective, sources, scope, exclusions, invariants, read/write ownership, deliverable, verification, and stop condition. Default to no full-history inheritance; include only targeted files/symbols and minimal recent turns when a recent decision is essential.

Readers are read-only unless explicitly authorized. One file has one writer. A child returns after two distinct evidence-based failed attempts, an ambiguity, a protected boundary, or completion. Worktrees start at budget zero and may be created only by the root under a finite permit for a verified isolation need.
