# Thesis Constraints

## Current academic scope

- Structural Engineering specialization.
- Expected thesis completion context: 2027.
- Current stage: topic selection and proposal preparation.

## Location and facilities

- Region III or Central Luzon is preferred for fieldwork and experiments.
- Nearby areas include Pampanga, Angeles, Mabalacat, Clark, Tarlac, Bulacan, and Nueva Ecija.
- More distant facilities may be considered when required testing is unavailable locally.

## Time and literature

- Actual experimental or data-gathering work should generally fit within approximately one to two months.
- December through January is preferable.
- February may be tight because of final-defense timing.
- Literature should generally prioritize the most recent five years unless foundational sources are necessary.

## Research quality

- Sustainability is allowed but is not a mandatory thesis priority.
- The study should produce measurable Structural Engineering behavior.
- Prefer member-, connection-, retrofit-, system-, or structural-performance-level work over generic cube- or cylinder-only studies.
- Feasibility, defensibility, standards, facilities, budget, time, and safety must be checked.
- Do not describe tentative thesis ideas as approved topics.
