The model ceilings are independent eligibility ceilings, not staffing targets. Sol
selects the smallest useful topology for the accepted task and may legitimately use no
workers or multiple bounded direct workers. Sol never spawns another Sol. Children may
not spawn. Total direct children remain bounded by the native session ceiling of sixteen.

Writer concurrency is independent from worker capacity. Two writers may coexist only
across proven-isolated repositories or worktrees with separate locks and no shared
pointer, migration, release file, generated artifact, provider resource, database, or
dependency race. A repository or worktree has at most one canonical writer.

continuation remain disabled. A fallback is a new explicit Sol routing decision allowed
by current governance; it is never provider-driven execution.
